# Privacy Policy
*Last Updated: August 4, 2026*

At Slim Storage Solutions LLC, the creators of **s3lim**, we are committed to protecting the privacy and security of your data. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and handle information when you visit our website, subscribe to s3lim via the AWS Marketplace, or install our software.

## 1. Zero-Egress Architecture (Our Core Principle)
s3lim is designed from the ground up as an **in-account, zero-egress serverless application**. 

* **In-Account Processing:** All processing of your S3 inventory files, object metadata, and bucket analytics is performed entirely within your own AWS account.
* **No Data Transmission:** No S3 data, storage metrics, prefix structures, file lists, or configuration data ever leave your AWS environment.
* **No Access:** We do not have access to, nor do we collect, copy, or store, your S3 bucket contents or analytical reports.

## 2. Information We Collect
While our software does not transmit your S3 data to us, we do collect limited information necessary to manage subscriptions, provide customer support, and maintain our business operations:

* **Marketplace Subscriber Information:** When you subscribe to s3lim via the AWS Marketplace, AWS provides us with technical identifiers (such as your AWS Account ID and Subscription ID) to enable licensing and billing verification.
* **Support Communications:** When you contact us for technical assistance (e.g., via support@slimstorage.io), we collect your name, email address, company name, and any information you choose to include in your inquiry.
* **Voluntary Contact Information:** We may collect your contact information (such as your name and email address) if you voluntarily register to receive updates, newsletters, product announcements, and release notes.
* **Website Usage:** We may collect standard web server logs and anonymous usage analytics to monitor and improve website performance.

## 3. How We Use Your Information
We use the limited information we collect strictly to:

* Verify and manage your s3lim subscription and license status.
* Respond to your support requests, inquiries, and customer service needs.
* Publish and distribute communications, updates, and releases regarding s3lim.
* Comply with AWS Marketplace seller terms and financial reporting obligations.

## 4. Sharing Your Information
We do not sell, trade, or share your contact or subscription information with third parties, except:

* With AWS, as necessary to process transactions, manage AWS Marketplace subscriptions, and resolve billing issues.
* As required by law, to comply with legal processes or protect the rights, property, or safety of Slim Storage Solutions LLC, our users, or the public.

## 5. Data Retention
We retain support email correspondence and Marketplace subscription identifiers only as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy or to satisfy regulatory requirements.
